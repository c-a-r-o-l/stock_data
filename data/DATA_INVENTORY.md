## 4. History_4_E_Alfred — Raw FRED Macro Downloads (140 files, 31 MB)

Generated by `src/4_download_macro.py`.  One CSV per economic series, downloaded
from the FRED/ALFRED APIs with two modes:

- **54 multi-revision files** (`_revision_timeline.csv`): contain
  `observation_date, revision_rank, revision_name, value, released_on` —
  ALFRED vintage history for revisable series (GDP, CPI, payrolls, etc.)
- **86 single-revision files** (`_latest.csv`): contain
  `observation_date, value` — latest-vintage-only for non-revisable series
  (yields, FX, SP500, etc.)

Covers all 140 series documented in `_ALFRED_MANIFEST.csv` across categories
A (Fed balance sheet), B (employment), C (inflation/GDP), D (production),
E (financial conditions), F (credit spreads), G (FX), H (commodities),
I (sentiment), J (housing), K (fiscal), L (equities), M (rates),
N (construction), O (money stock), R (OECD CLI), T (national accounts).

## 5. History_4_E_Alfred_Release — Macro With Release Dates (140 files, 35 MB)

Generated by `src/4_download_macro_release.py`.  Copies all files from
`History_4_E_Alfred/` and computes a `release_date` column where missing,
using the documented release delays in `_ALFRED_MANIFEST.csv`.

**Two formats:**
- **Multi-revision** (54 files): `observation_date, revision_rank, revision_name, value, released_on` — ALFRED data with real vintage tracking
- **Single-revision** (86 files): `observation_date, value, release_date` — calculated release dates based on frequency + documented delay rules (e.g., monthly data → observation + 30 days + lag)

The calculated release dates are approximate but span the full history
(1919–2026), unlike the raw ALFRED vintages which bunch pre-2016 data at
2016-01-01.  **This is the preferred source for macro data.**

## 6. History_4_F — Raw Fundamental Data (404 files, 42 MB)

Quarterly fundamental data sourced from financial statements.  One `.txt` per
symbol (CSV format, CYYMMDD dates, 46 columns).

**Columns:** MRQ (Most Recent Quarter) and TTM (Trailing Twelve Months) for:
Total Long-Term Debt, Total Equity, Total Debt, Gross Profit, Net Income,
Operating Income, Revenue, EPS, Gross/Operating Margin, Total Assets/Liabilities,
Current Assets/Liabilities, Long-Term Assets/Liabilities, Net Interest,
Depreciation, Amortization, CapEx, Operating Cash Flow, Cash & Equivalents,
Shares Outstanding.  Plus valuation ratios: PE, ROE, LT_DE, EPS_Growth,
Revenue_Growth.

**Known issues:** 30 symbols have all-zero data.  18.4% of rows are all-zero
(padding).  176 files have duplicate dates.  MRQ_Net_Interest/TTM_Net_Interest
are zero for 397/404 symbols (only 7 banks have non-zero values).
