### Event 1: Bull Steepener ★★☆

| Field | Content |
|------|------|
| Trigger Condition | Both 2Y and 10Y yields decline simultaneously, but the short end (2Y) declines faster. Quantified: 2s10s spread widens >10bp, and 2Y decline > 10Y decline |
| Macro Implication | Market prices rate cut expectations (short end drops rapidly) + concerns about long-term economic outlook (long end drops but by less). "The economy is slowing to the point where the Fed has to cut rates" |
| Impact on Equities | Historically the worst yield curve regime. NDR research: S&P 500 performs weakest under this regime. Last occurrence: Aug 2019 – Aug 2020 (includes COVID shock) |
| Style Factor Impact | Defensive > Cyclical, High Quality > Low Quality, Large Cap > Small Cap |
| Logic Chain | Growth slows → market prices recession risk → Fed forced to cut → short-end rates plunge → but long end also falls on pessimism → Bull Steepener → equities decline (pricing policy error risk + growth scare) |
| Failure Scenarios | If rate cuts successfully avert recession (e.g., 1995 "soft landing" cuts), equities may rise instead of fall |
| Data Requirements | M_DGS2_2Y_Treasury.csv + M_DGS10_10Y_Treasury.csv → compute 2s10s spread change | ✅ Computable |

### Event 6: 10Y-3M Yield Curve Inversion (Estrella-Mishkin Core Indicator) ★★★

| Field | Content |
|------|------|
| Trigger Condition | 3M T-bill yield > 10Y Treasury yield (i.e., 10Y-3M spread < 0) |
| Statistical Pattern | Using monthly averages: since 1968, zero false positives. All 6 NBER recessions saw at least 3 consecutive months of negative spread within the prior 12 months. Estrella-Mishkin probability model: spread -0.82% → recession probability 50%; -2.40% → recession probability 90% |
| Comparison with 10Y-2Y | Bauer & Mertens (2018b): "10Y-3M consistently outperforms 10Y-2Y by a noticeable margin." The Fed's officially preferred spread indicator |
| Lead Time | Predictive power strongest at 2-6 quarters (6-18 months) ahead |

| ★Threshold Optimization (v2.2) | Chicago Fed Letter 425 (Kelley 2019): Traditional zero threshold (inverted) correctly classifies only 57% of recession months, false positive rate 5%. ### Framework: Priority Ranking of Macro Contradictions

When multiple macro signals conflict, judge the dominant contradiction by the following hierarchy:

| Priority | Signal Category | Reason | Citation |
|--------|---------|------|------|
| 1 (Highest) | Yield Curve Regime (10Y-3M / 10Y-2Y) | Synthesizes monetary policy expectations + growth expectations + inflation expectations. The market's optimal forecast of the future | Estrella & Mishkin; Dalio; Druckenmiller |
| 2 | Credit Spreads (HY OAS / IG OAS) | Directly reflects corporate actual financing costs, default risk, and credit market health. Leads equities by 1-3 months | BofA; Howard Marks; Fridson |
| 3 | Financial Conditions (NFCI / STLFSI4) | Synthesizes 105/18 financial indicators, reflects overall financial system stress. Leads GDP by 1-2 quarters | Chicago Fed; St. Louis Fed |
| 4 | Fed Policy Direction (Hike/Cut/QE/QT) | Affects liquidity volume and marginal price, but long lags and endogeneity exist | Bernanke; Dalio |
| 5 | Labor Market (Sahm Rule / Claims / Payrolls) | The best "Ground Truth" for confirming or refuting recession. Claims lead, Payrolls lag | Sahm; Conference Board |
| 6 | PMI / New Orders | Purchasing managers first perceive demand changes. New Orders is the most leading indicator of the industrial cycle | ISM; Conference Board LEI |
| 7 | Inflation (Core PCE / 5Y5Y / Breakeven) | Determines Fed policy space. Inflation is a constraint, not a directional signal | Fed; Dalio |
| 8 | Dollar (Broad Dollar / DXY) | Amplifier of global financial conditions. Third-layer variable from US perspective | IMF; FRB NY |
| 9 | Commodity Prices (Oil / Copper/Gold) | Provides supplementary information on supply-demand balance and risk appetite. Oil is one of the few variables that can independently trigger recession | Hamilton; Kilian |
| 10 | Valuation (CAPE / Buffett Indicator / ERP) | Determines long-term return rates (3-10 years), but has almost no predictive power for <1-year direction | Shiller; Buffett; Damodaran |


### Conflict Resolution Rules

Rule 1 — Credit Spreads vs. PMI:
  Spreads widening + PMI still high → Credit market leads, follow the spread signal.
  Source: Credit investors bear default risk first, perceive turning points earlier than purchasing managers.

Rule 2 — Yield Curve vs. Employment:
  Curve inverted + employment still strong → Follow the curve (leads by 16 months). Employment is lagging.
  Source: Estrella & Mishkin (1996); Conference Board LEI.

Rule 3 — NFCI vs. Single Indicators:
  NFCI >0 but individual indicators still good → Trust the NFCI signal. The aggregated signal of 105
  financial indicators is stronger than any single indicator. Source: Chicago Fed.

Rule 4 — Extreme Valuation + Low Volatility (Most Dangerous Combination):
  2018 Jan (CAPE 33 + VIX<10), 2021 Nov (CAPE 38 + VIX<15).
  When valuations are extreme + volatility is very low → fragility peaks → a moderate catalyst
